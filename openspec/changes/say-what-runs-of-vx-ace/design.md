## Context

The requirement "State of VX Ace once its games start" was written by the change
`run-rgss3-games-in-a-guest-of-their-own`, when the classes of a game of VX Ace were those of
RGSS2. The binding changes of 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-14 replaced them one by one.

## Goals / Non-Goals

Goals: the documents say what runs. Non-goals: declaring a milestone; anything a comparison
with the original would have to show.

## Decisions

- VX Ace stays experimental: the rows A1 to A3 need a comparison with the original, which the
  change `run-the-oracle-on-vx-ace-games` makes possible.
- The documents do not name the games of the corpus as running; they say what a game made
  with the standard scripts does, as the measured run showed, and that no frame was compared.
