# Scenes of windows of RGSS2 with the pictures of the original runtime of VX: a selection of the
# case files of the window measurement of 2026-10-12 (RGSS202E.dll 2.0.2.1; the file of version
# 2.0.0.1 gave the same pictures). The format is that of `vkrgss_core::test_support::window_cases`.
#
# The digest of a frame is that of the picture the original drew. In all of these but two it is
# also that of the picture the model of that measurement draws. In `g8-smallskin_t_0` every
# skin, and in `g6-skin_sizes_t` the skin of 1 by 1, is narrower or lower than 64 pixels: of such
# a skin the original draws less of the background than that model does. The rule was found by
# the window measurement of RGSS3 of 2026-10-13 (a stretched block that leaves the skin is cut
# and its target shortened), and with it the model draws the pictures of the original of VX for
# these two as well.
#
# By rule of the design of the change `draw-the-window-of-rgss2`:
#   1, 2    background with its pattern, frame   g1 (sizes, opacities), g17 (overlapping pieces)
#   3       blending, odd columns                g4-vp_clip, g6-edges
#   4       openness                             g3, g5, g6-huge_open
#   5, 6    contents, cursor                     g2-contents, g2-cursor, g9-blink
#   7, 8    pause sign, arrows                   g2-arrows_pause, g9-opacities_front
#   9       latched state                        g10, g12, g13, g14, g15, g16
#   10      one element, viewports, sizes        g4-vp_z, g6-small, g6-no_skin, g6-skin_sizes,
#                                                g6-brightness, g8
